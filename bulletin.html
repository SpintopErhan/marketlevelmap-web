Structure

Whether the market is making higher highs (up) or lower lows (down). BOS: the trend continues past its last swing. CHoCH: the protected level broke and the trend turned. Sweep: a wick ran past a range edge and the candle closed back inside. AVWAP: the volume-weighted average price traded since the protected swing; price holding above it means buyers since that point are on average in profit.
