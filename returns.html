Monthly returns

Every month's change from its first open to its last close, year by year.

Loading the monthly returns…

Monthly return: the month's first daily open to its last daily close (UTC). Year: the year's first open to its last close. The current month and year are still running (dashed). A first month whose data starts after the 1st is left out. Average, median and positive months use the completed months only. Macro and commodities (Yahoo Finance): the previous month's close to the month's close; S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 are the cash indices, gold and silver the COMEX futures (free sources have no long spot history; the monthly change is close to spot's). The last 10 years are shown. Past returns describe what happened; they say nothing about what comes next.
