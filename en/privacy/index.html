Privacy policy

You can use MarketLevelMap without an account; then we collect no personal data from you. This page explains which data we process when you open an account, why, and what your rights are.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

MarketLevelMap (marketlevelmap.com) is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at marketlevelmap@gmail.com or through the @marketlevelmap account on X.

Using the site without an account

No account is needed to browse the site. In that case:

  • No cookie, tracking, advertising or visitor-statistics tool is used to identify you.
  • Your language, theme and favourite assets are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Live prices and candles are fetched by your browser directly from market data providers (Binance, Coinbase), and the chart library is loaded from a content delivery network (unpkg). Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, briefly processes technical data such as your IP address in its own systems to serve the site and protect it from abuse; we do not use it to track visitors.

What we collect when you open an account

  • From your Google account: your Google account ID, e-mail address, name and profile picture address. We do not receive your Google password.
  • What you enter: your X username and, if you wish, your Telegram username.
  • What we create: your verification code, your membership status (with a short note if an application is rejected or a membership suspended), the version and date of the consent you gave, and the dates your account was created, updated and approved.

Why, and on what legal basis

To create and run your account, to verify membership by matching your X account to your account once, and to prevent one person from opening several accounts. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling, and nothing is sold.

The legal basis is the explicit consent you give when you create your account and the performance of the membership you request (GDPR article 6(1)(a) and (b); KVKK article 5).

Where it is kept and who receives it

Data is stored on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) infrastructure, whose servers may be outside your country; your consent covers this transfer. Sign-in is provided by Google. Your data is not shared with anyone else, except with the competent authorities where the law requires it.

Cookies

Only when you sign in, cookies needed for the account to work are used:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Sign-in hint cookie: lets pages know you are signed in; it holds no personal data.
  • Short-lived security cookies: protect the Google sign-in and account creation steps and expire within minutes.

No tracking, analytics or advertising cookies are used.

How long

Until you delete your account. When you do, your data is deleted; only your X username is kept for 30 days, to stop it from being registered again right away, and then it is removed too.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data on your account page and delete your account there at any time. Under the GDPR and KVKK article 11 you can also ask which data is processed, request correction or deletion, object to processing and withdraw your consent. Write to marketlevelmap@gmail.com for these requests; we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with its new date. If the data we process changes substantially, account holders are asked for their consent again.
